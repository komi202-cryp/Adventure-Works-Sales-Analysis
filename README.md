# 🚲 Adventure Works Cycles - Sales & Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
Adventure Works Cycles is a multinational manufacturing company producing bicycles, components, and accessories. This project provides an end-to-end analytical solution covering data integration, SQL transformations, advanced Excel modeling, and interactive Power BI & Tableau dashboards.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, lookup models (`XLOOKUP`, `VLOOKUP`), fiscal date hierarchy, and Pivot Tables.
- **SQL:** Data manipulation, aggregations, and business metrics queries.
- **Power BI & Tableau:** Interactive visual storytelling, KPI scorecards, and multi-dimensional slicers.

---

## 📂 Key Business Questions Answered
1. **Sales & Profitability:** Calculating net sales amount, production costs, and overall profit margins.
2. **Date & Calendar Intelligence:** Monthly, quarterly, and fiscal year performance tracking (July-start financial year).
3. **Product & Customer Segmentation:** Identifying top revenue-generating bike models and high-value customer demographics.
4. **Regional Insights:** Performance across North American, European, and Asian commercial markets.
